INVITATION FOR PREQUALIFICATION
PQ No.: GP/MoYS/PQ/W-01/2083-84
Date of publication: 2083-07-07 B.S. (2026-10-24 A.D.)

1. The Ministry of Youth and Sports, Gandaki Province, intends to prequalify contractors for the construction of a Multi-purpose Covered Sports Hall (3,000 seats, steel truss roof, with parking and site works) at Rangasala, Pokhara-16.

2. The approximate cost of the works is NPR 32 crore. Only prequalified applicants will be invited to bid.

3. Prequalification criteria: construction licence of Class "A" or "B"; average annual construction turnover of NPR 40,00,00,000 over the best three of the last ten years; at least one building with long-span steel structure of NPR 15,00,00,000 completed in the last ten years; key personnel and equipment as per the PQ document.

4. Joint ventures of up to three partners may apply; partners will be jointly and severally liable.

5. Applications must be submitted through the PPMO e-GP system no later than 12:00 hours on 2083-09-10 B.S. (2026-12-25 A.D.). No bid security is required at the prequalification stage.
