INVITATION FOR BIDS
ICB No.: NEA/DCSD/ICB/G-07/083-84
Date of first publication: 28 October 2026

1. Nepal Electricity Authority invites electronic bids from eligible manufacturers for the Design, Manufacture, Testing, Supply and Delivery of 11/0.4 kV Distribution Transformers: 750 nos. of 100 kVA, 400 nos. of 200 kVA and 100 nos. of 300 kVA, with type test reports from an independent laboratory.

2. Estimated cost: not disclosed.

3. Eligibility: manufacturers who have manufactured and supplied at least 2,000 distribution transformers of 100 kVA or above in the last five years, with satisfactory performance certificates from at least two utilities. Bidders that are not manufacturers are not eligible.

4. Domestic preference: a margin of preference shall be applied in favour of transformers manufactured in Nepal, as per the bidding document.

5. Bids must be submitted through the PPMO e-GP system no later than 12:00 hours on 2 December 2026. Bids will be opened at 14:00 hours on the same day.

6. Bid security: NPR 1,20,00,000 or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency, valid until 28 days beyond the bid validity period of 120 days.

7. Joint ventures are not permitted. Bid document fee: NPR 15,000.
