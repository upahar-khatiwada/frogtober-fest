Date of First Publication: 2083-07-01 B.S. (2026-10-18 A.D.)

IFB No.: GP/MoPID/IDO-K/NCB/W-02/2083-84
Package: SRT-02 - Construction of Seti River Embankment and River Training Works at Pokhara-17 (1.2 km)

1. The Infrastructure Development Office, Kaski, under the Ministry of Physical Infrastructure Development, Gandaki Province, invites sealed electronic bids from eligible contractors for the construction of reinforced concrete embankment, launching apron, spurs and gabion revetment along 1.2 km of the Seti River.

2. Estimated cost of the works: NPR 18.50 crore (excluding VAT and contingencies). Intended completion period: 18 months.

3. Minimum qualification criteria:
   (a) Contractors holding a valid construction licence of Class "A" or "B" only.
   (b) Average annual construction turnover of the last three fiscal years (2080/81, 2081/82 and 2082/83) of not less than NPR 25,00,00,000.
   (c) Specific experience of at least one river training, embankment or bank protection work of value not less than NPR 9,00,00,000 in a single contract completed within the last seven fiscal years.
   (d) Liquid assets and/or credit facilities of not less than NPR 3,70,00,000.
   (e) Availability of key equipment: 3 excavators (min 0.9 m3), 1 batching plant (30 m3/hr), 1 transit mixer, 2 dewatering pumps.
   (f) Company registration, VAT/PAN registration and tax clearance certificate for F/Y 2082/83.

4. Joint venture is NOT permitted for this contract. Bids submitted by a joint venture shall be treated as non-responsive.

5. Bidding documents may be obtained from the PPMO e-GP portal on payment of a non-refundable fee of NPR 5,000.

6. Electronic bids must be submitted through the e-GP system no later than 12:00 noon on 2083-08-15 B.S. (2026-12-01 A.D.). Bids will be opened at 13:00 on the same day.

7. Bid security of NPR 55,50,000 in the form of an unconditional bank guarantee, valid for at least 150 days from the bid submission deadline, must accompany each bid. Bids shall remain valid for 120 days.

8. Performance security: 5% of the contract price.
