REQUEST FOR EXPRESSION OF INTEREST (EOI)
Consulting Services for Detailed Project Report (DPR) of 12 Motorable Bridges in Gandaki Province

EOI No.: DoLI/EOI/CS-11/2083-84
Date of publication: 2083-07-05 B.S. (2026-10-22 A.D.)

1. The Department of Local Infrastructure (DoLI) intends to apply a portion of its F/Y 2083/84 budget to eligible payments under a contract for consulting services for the detailed survey, design, cost estimate and DPR preparation of 12 motorable bridges (spans 30-120 m) on local roads in Kaski, Tanahun, Syangja and Myagdi districts.

2. DoLI now invites eligible national consulting firms to indicate their interest in providing the services. Interested consultants must provide information demonstrating that they are qualified to perform the services: firm registration, PAN/VAT registration, tax clearance or tax return evidence for F/Y 2082/83, general and specific experience (bridge design and DPR of similar scale in the last 7 years), and the qualifications of key experts (bridge engineer, geotechnical engineer, hydrologist, highway engineer).

3. EOI evaluation criteria: qualification of the firm 10%, experience of the firm 40%, capacity of the firm 20%, key experts 30%. Minimum score for shortlisting: 60%.

4. Consultants may associate with other firms in the form of a joint venture or sub-consultancy to enhance their qualifications. The maximum number of JV partners is three.

5. A consultant will be selected in accordance with the Quality and Cost Based Selection (QCBS) method set out in the Public Procurement Rules; the shortlisted firms will receive a Request for Proposals.

6. Expressions of interest must be submitted electronically through the PPMO e-GP system on or before 2083-08-10 B.S. (2026-11-26 A.D.) by 12:00 hours.

Director General, Department of Local Infrastructure
