INVITATION FOR BIDS (बोलपत्र आह्वानको सूचना)
(First date of publication: 2083-07-02 B.S.)

Contract Identification No.: BM/NCB/W-07/2083-84
Name of Work: Upgrading and Gravelling of Bhanu-Satiswara Road (3.2 km) including Side Drains and Slab Culverts, Bhanu-6, Tanahun

1. Bhanu Municipality, Office of the Municipal Executive, invites electronic bids from eligible bidders for the above work under the National Competitive Bidding (NCB) procedure, funded from the municipal budget of F/Y 2083/84.

2. Estimated cost (excluding VAT): Rs. 1,48,75,000/-
   Scope: earthwork in road formation, 150 mm gravel sub-base and base course over 3.2 km, 1.8 km of stone-masonry side drain, 4 nos. RCC slab culverts (span 2-4 m), and road safety signs.
   Time for completion: 240 days from the date of contract agreement.

3. Eligible bidders may download the bidding document from the PPMO e-GP system (www.bolpatra.gov.np/egp) after paying a non-refundable fee of Rs. 3,000/- to the municipality's revenue account (Nepal Bank Ltd., Damauli Branch, A/C No. 0000-DEMO-0001), and must upload the payment voucher with the bid.

4. Bids must be submitted electronically through the PPMO e-GP system on or before 12:00 hours on 2083-08-05 B.S. (2026-11-21 A.D.). Bids received after this deadline shall be rejected. Bids will be opened at 14:00 hours on the same day at the office of the municipality in the presence of bidders or their representatives who choose to attend.

5. Bid security: Bidders must submit a bid security of Rs. 4,46,000/- in the form of a bank guarantee from an "A" class commercial bank, valid until 2083-12-15 B.S., or a cash deposit to the municipality's deposit account. Bid validity: 90 days from the bid submission deadline.

6. Qualification requirements (summary; see Section III of the bidding document):
   a) Valid firm/company registration certificate and construction licence of Class "D" or above, renewed for F/Y 2083/84.
   b) VAT and PAN registration certificate.
   c) Tax clearance certificate of F/Y 2082/83, or evidence of tax return submission for F/Y 2082/83.
   d) Average annual construction turnover of the best three years within the last five fiscal years of at least Rs. 1,50,00,000.
   e) Successful completion of at least one similar road work (gravel or blacktop road) of value not less than Rs. 60,00,000 in a single contract within the last five fiscal years.
   f) Self-declaration that the bidder is not blacklisted and has no conflict of interest.
   g) Key personnel: one civil engineer and one sub-engineer; key equipment: one excavator, one roller (8-10 t), two tippers.

7. Joint venture: Bidders may form a joint venture of not more than three partners. The lead partner shall hold at least 40% share. A registered JV agreement must be submitted with the bid and the bid security must be in the name of all partners.

8. If the last date of bid submission or opening falls on a public holiday, the deadline shall be the next working day at the same time.

9. The municipality reserves the right to accept or reject any or all bids without assigning any reason.
