ICB No.: DoR/ICB/2083-84/01
Contract: Upgrading of Kanti Lokpath, km 12+000 to km 38+500, to Two-Lane Asphalt Concrete Standard, including Slope Stabilisation and Cross Drainage Structures

1. The Government of Nepal has allocated funds from its own resources towards the cost of the above project. The Department of Roads now invites sealed electronic bids from eligible and qualified bidders.

2. Approximate estimated cost: NPR 2,400 million (excluding VAT).

3. Bidding will be conducted through International Competitive Bidding procedures as specified in the Public Procurement Act, 2063 and Public Procurement Rules, 2064, and is open to all eligible bidders. Bidding documents are in English.

4. Qualification (summary): average annual construction turnover of NPR 3,600 million over the best three of the last ten years; at least two similar road works of NPR 1,200 million each completed in the last ten years; key personnel and equipment as per Section III. Domestic bidders must hold a Class "A" construction licence.

5. Joint ventures are permitted with a maximum of three partners. The lead partner must meet at least 40% and each other partner at least 25% of the financial criteria. Domestic preference shall apply as specified in the bidding document.

6. Bids must be submitted electronically through the PPMO e-GP system on or before January 11, 2027 at 12:00 hours (Nepal Standard Time). Technical envelopes will be opened at 14:00 hours on the same day.

7. Bid security: NPR 48,000,000 or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency, valid for 30 days beyond the bid validity of 120 days.

8. Bid document fee: NPR 25,000.
