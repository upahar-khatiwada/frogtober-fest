Name of Contract: Supply, Delivery, Installation and Commissioning of ICU Ventilators (40 units), Multiparameter Patient Monitors (120 units) and Syringe Infusion Pumps (200 units)

1. The Government of Nepal has allocated funds in the fiscal year budget towards the cost of the above goods. The Department of Health Services invites electronic bids from eligible manufacturers or their authorized agents for the supply, delivery to the Central Medical Store, Pathlaiya, installation, user training and three years comprehensive warranty.

2. Approximate cost estimate: NPR 9,24,00,000 (inclusive of all taxes, customs duties and delivery).

3. Bidding will be conducted through International Competitive Bidding procedures in accordance with the Public Procurement Act and Rules of Nepal, and is open to all eligible bidders. Bids must be prepared in English.

4. Qualification requirements include: manufacturer's authorization; ISO 13485 and CE or US FDA certificate for each offered model; supply of at least 25 ICU ventilators of the offered make in each of the last three years; average annual turnover of not less than NPR 18,00,00,000 over the last three years; and availability of an after-sales service centre in Nepal.

5. A margin of preference for goods manufactured in Nepal shall apply as specified in the bidding document.

6. Bids must be submitted through the e-GP system (www.bolpatra.gov.np/egp) on or before December 14, 2026, 12:00 hours Nepal Standard Time. Late bids shall be rejected. Bids will be opened on the same day at 14:00 hours.

7. All bids must be accompanied by a bid security of NPR 18,50,000 or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency, valid for 148 days from the bid submission deadline.

8. Bid document fee: NPR 10,000 (non-refundable).
